Dan Dongseong Kim

Dr. Dan DongSeong Kim is Deputy Director of UQ Cyber Security and an Associate Professor (Reader in the commonwealth system) in Cybersecurity at The University of Queensland (UQ), Brisbane, Australia. He leads the Dependable and Secure systems Lab (DSL). in the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) at UQ. Dr Kim belongs to Cyber Security and Software Engineering (CSS) discipline in School of EECS at UQ. His research interests are in cyber security modeling and analysis for various systems and networks including AI, IoT/CPS, Autonomous Vehicles, Maritime Vessles, Drones, Satellite Systems, Cloud, and software-defined networking (SDN).
