Applying for a Higher Degree by Research (HDR) Program

Before beginning an HDR program, such as a Master of Philosophy (MPhil) or PhD, you must be formally admitted to UQ. Information about the application process and entry requirements is available on the UQ HDR application page.

Funding

Applying for HDR Supervision

Before I can comment on whether I may be able to supervise you, please combine the following documents into one PDF file and email it to dan [dot] kim [at] uq [dot] edu [dot] au.

  1. Your CV or résumé in English.
  2. Your English-language test result, such as IELTS or TOEFL, if applicable. Please check the UQ English-language requirements.
  3. Your academic transcript(s), cumulative GPA, and class or department ranking, if available.
    • Example: GPA of 3.0 out of 4.5 for a bachelor's degree.
    • Example: GPA of 6.3 out of 7.0 for a master's degree.
  4. Any published or accepted research papers, technical reports, or other relevant research outputs.
  5. Your funding plan, such as self-funding, government funding, or external funding.

    Please state your funding plan clearly. I do not usually fund HDR students. UQ scholarship opportunities are highly competitive; a strong academic record and prior research experience may strengthen an application.

  6. Your research interests. Please be as specific as possible.

Paper Review and Coding Test

If your background and research interests align with mine, the selection process may include the following steps:

  1. Research paper review: I will provide a paper related to our shared research interests. Please download the review form and return your review within one week.
  2. Coding test: You may be asked to complete a coding task to demonstrate your programming skills.
  3. Interview: An interview may be offered to applicants who pass the paper review and coding test.
